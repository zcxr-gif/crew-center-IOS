After you join

What being a member actually involves

No check-ride and nothing to be signed off for. You join, you fly one sector for the airline, and you log it — that is the induction. Everything below is the Operations Plan, in the order you will meet it.

Your first seven days

One logged sector inside seven days of being accepted. Any published route counts, at Cadet rank and inside the three-hour Cadet limit. It is the flight report that stops the clock, once staff have validated it.

A warning goes out two days before the deadline, so nobody is closed without notice — and anyone closed this way may apply again with nothing held against them.

Staying current

One validated flight every 30 days. The crew centre tracks it and acts on it automatically — it is not a rule somebody has to remember to apply, and a warning goes out seven days before.

Miss it and you move to the inactive roster. Nobody is removed for it: your hours, rank and flight history are kept, and you are active again the moment you log a flight.

When life gets busy

Leave of absence is granted on request for up to 90 days and pauses the activity clock. Rank and hours are held exactly where you left them.

Line Trainers are there for the first few sectors, for remedial help, and for anyone coming back after a while away.

Filing a flight

Fly under an Aeromexico callsign on the Expert Server in a fleet aircraft and the crew centre captures the leg, matches the route and credits it. You can file by hand if something is missed.

Reports are filed within 48 hours of landing and validated within 72, with the flight number, aircraft, block time and evidence of the arrival.

Getting promoted

Automatically, on validated hours. Each rank releases more of the fleet and raises the longest sector you may file — from three hours at Cadet to no limit at all at Senior Captain.

Questions

Before you ask

I am Grade 2. Can I still apply?

Yes — Grade 2 is our bar to apply. Infinite Flight itself requires Grade 3 to enter the Expert Server, which is where we fly, so you will need to reach it before your first line sector. Apply now and get there with us.

Can I keep flying for another VA?

Yes. We don't ask for exclusivity. Just don't fly our callsign for someone else's operation. Hours transferred in from another VA are recognised up to 50, on evidence, and don't count towards Captain or above.

How do hours get credited?

Automatically. Fly under an Aeromexico callsign on the Expert Server in a fleet aircraft and the crew center captures the leg, matches the route and credits it once a validator has approved the report.

What if I'm declined?

You'll be told why, and whether you can reapply. Most declines are grade or an outstanding violation — both fixable in a couple of weeks.
