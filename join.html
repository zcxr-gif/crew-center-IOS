Questions

Before you ask

Do I need to fly a minimum every month?

One logged sector every 30 days keeps you current. Tell us if life gets busy and we'll park your account rather than remove it.

Can I keep flying for another VA?

Yes. We don't ask for exclusivity. Just don't fly our callsign for someone else's operation.

How do hours get credited?

Automatically. Fly under an Aeromexico callsign on the Expert Server in a fleet aircraft and the crew center captures the leg, matches the route and credits it. You can file by hand if something is missed.

What if I'm declined?

You'll be told why, and whether you can reapply. Most declines are hours or violations — both fixable in a couple of weeks.
