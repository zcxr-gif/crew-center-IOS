The fleet

Six types, and a job for each

The fleet is drawn from the real Aeroméxico operation and limited to types available in Infinite Flight in an Aeroméxico livery. No paper fleet, and no types you cannot load into the sim tonight.

Its strength is the spread. Two regional and narrowbody types carry the domestic trunk and the US transborder network and are what a new pilot flies from day one; two Dreamliners carry Europe, South America and the Pacific; the 777 is kept for events. Every type is released at a rank, so the fleet opens up as you fly rather than sitting there as a catalogue.
