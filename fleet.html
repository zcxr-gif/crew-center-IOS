The fleet

Six types, one livery

We only operate aircraft that exist in Infinite Flight wearing an Aeroméxico livery. No paper fleet, no types you can't actually load into the sim.

Why the names look like that

Fleet names are matching keys

Each type below is written exactly as Infinite Flight's API names it — "Boeing 787-9 Dreamliner", not "789". The Inflight Crew Center matches your live flight to our fleet on that string to credit the hours automatically. Fly the right type in the right livery and the logbook takes care of itself.
