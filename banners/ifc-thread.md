![Quiénes somos · About|690x155](https://aeromexicova.org/banners/about.png)

Aeromexico Virtual is a virtual airline for Infinite Flight pilots who want to fly Aeroméxico the way it is actually flown. Our routes, aircraft and procedures follow the real airline wherever the simulator allows, and everything we publish comes from our Operations Plan — no invented numbers, no paper fleet.

**What we hold ourselves to**

* :airplane: **Realism first** — routes, aircraft and procedures follow the real airline unless the simulator makes that impossible.
* :busts_in_silhouette: **Members before numbers** — a roster of active pilots who enjoy flying is the goal. Headcount is a by-product, not the target.
* :handshake: **Staff serve the roster** — every staff role exists to remove work from pilots, not to add rank.
* :loudspeaker: **Say what is decided** — decisions, rule changes and disciplinary outcomes are communicated in plain terms, promptly.

![Rangos · Ranks|690x155](https://aeromexicova.org/banners/ranks.png)

Hours unlock aircraft, and longer sectors come with them. Promotion is automatic: it is applied when the flight report that crosses the threshold is validated.

| Rank | Hours | Sector limit | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cadet | 0 | 3h | E190, 737-800 |
| Second Officer | 10 | 5h | 737 MAX 8 |
| First Officer | 40 | 7.5h | 787-8 |
| Senior First Officer | 80 | 10h | 787-9 |
| Captain | 200 | 13h | 777-200ER · full fleet |
| Senior Captain | 500 | None | Ultra-long-range codeshares |
| Aeroméxico Airman | Appointed | None | 1.4× hour multiplier |

[details="What each rank gets you"]
**Cadet** — Where every pilot starts. One sector in your first seven days and you are a line pilot.
* E190 and 737-800 — the Connect and domestic fleet, released on day one.
* A Line Trainer on call — for your first sectors, and for as long after as you want one.
* The whole crew centre — logbook, roster, route booking and automatic flight reports.

**Second Officer** — The transborder network opens up, and with it the first sectors into the United States.
* The 737 MAX 8 — and with it the whole US transborder network.
* Event sign-ups open — any group departure inside your sector limit.
* A say in the network — put a route to Flight Operations and it gets considered.

**First Officer** — Widebody flying begins. The 787-8 and the deeper South American network.
* The 787-8 — your first widebody, and the South American long-haul with it.
* Nominated event crew — a seat on the flight rather than a place in the ballot.
* Eligible for staff — you may stand for a director post or a Line Trainer seat.

**Senior First Officer** — The flagship. Madrid, Amsterdam and the transatlantic network on the 787-9.
* The 787-9 flagship — Madrid, Amsterdam and the transatlantic network.
* First call on gates — you pick your stand and slot at a hub takeover.
* Named as a mentor — newer pilots are assigned to you on group flights.

**Captain** — Command of the long-haul fleet, and the full fleet released.
* The full fleet — every type released, the 777-200ER included.
* Command of group departures — you lead them when Flight Operations asks.
* Sign-off authority — you may clear a Cadet's first sector when no trainer is free.

**Senior Captain** — No sector limit at all — Tokyo, Seoul and the ultra-long-range codeshares.
* Event command — plan and run one of your own with the Director of Events.
* A standing voice — network proposals come to you at the monthly staff meeting.

**Aeroméxico Airman** — Awarded, not earned by hours. Made by the CEO on the Director of Flight Operations' recommendation, for sustained contribution.
* The crest in place of the bars — full fleet, and no sector limit.
* A 1.4× multiplier on every hour logged after the appointment.
* First refusal on charter, anniversary and joint-VA flying.
[/details]

[details="Rank rules"]
* The sector limit is a filed block time. A pilot may not book or file a route longer than their rank allows, and Flight Operations will not validate one.
* Codeshare flying is bound by the same limit — partner routes reach further than the Aeroméxico network alone, so this is where most pilots first meet it.
* Promotion is automatic on reaching the hour threshold, and is applied when the flight report that crosses it is validated.
* Hours transferred from another virtual airline are recognised up to 50 hours on production of evidence, and do not count towards Captain or above.
[/details]

![Únete · How to Join|690x155](https://aeromexicova.org/banners/join.png)

**Requirements**

* :white_check_mark: An active **Infinite Flight Pro** subscription — we fly the Expert Server.
* :white_check_mark: **Grade 2** or above to apply (Grade 3 is needed to fly the Expert Server line).
* :white_check_mark: No active violations or suspensions on the IFC.
* :white_check_mark: Not on the IFVARB Blacklist or Watchlist.
* :white_check_mark: An Infinite Flight Community account.
* :white_check_mark: Agreement to the Operations Plan and code of conduct.

### :point_right: [Apply at aeromexicova.org/apply](https://aeromexicova.org/apply)

It takes about three minutes, and the Director of Personnel answers every application within **72 hours**.

[details="What happens after you apply"]
1. We check your grade, violations and the IFVARB lists.
2. You get your answer within 72 hours, with your crew centre login.
3. You start as a **Cadet** on the E190 and 737-800 — fly one sector in your first seven days and you're on the line.
4. A Line Trainer is on hand for your first flights, and for as long after as you want one.
[/details]
