Why fly with us

An airline, not a spreadsheet

Everything a pilot needs sits in one crew center — logbook, roster, route network and events. You fly; the paperwork files itself.

Flights log themselves

Fly under an Aeromexico callsign on the Expert Server and the crew center captures the leg, matches it to the fleet and route network, and credits the hours. No forms unless something needs fixing.

A network worth flying

The real Aeroméxico map, published in five tiers — regional Connect hops on the E190, transborder sectors on the MAX, and 787 flagship runs to Europe and the Pacific.

Rank that means something

Each rank releases more of the fleet and raises the longest sector you may file — three hours at Cadet, no limit at Senior Captain. Promotions come off validated hours, not vibes.

Events with ATC

At least two a month, published a fortnight ahead, run with staffed controllers, published slots and a proper briefing — not a Discord message and a hope.

Staffed by people who fly

Our staff are line pilots first, and every one of them is named on the staff page. Applications get a human answer inside 72 hours, and every decision is explained.

Run to a written plan

How the airline is governed, what is expected of a pilot and how flights are validated are all written down, published, and reviewed every quarter. We operate in compliance with the IFVARB.
