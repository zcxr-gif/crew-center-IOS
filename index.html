Why fly with us

An airline, not a spreadsheet

Everything a pilot needs sits in one crew center — logbook, roster, route network and events. You fly; the paperwork files itself.

Flights log themselves

Fly under an Aeromexico callsign on the Expert Server and the crew center captures the leg, matches it to the fleet and route network, and credits the hours. No forms unless something needs fixing.

A network worth flying

23 destinations built on the real Aeroméxico map — regional Connect hops on the E190, transcons on the MAX, and 787 flagship sectors to Europe and the Pacific.

Rank that means something

Seven ranks from Cadet to Line Check Captain, each unlocking real fleet and route access. Promotions come off logged hours, not vibes.

Events with ATC

Group departures and fly-ins run with staffed controllers, published slots and a proper briefing — not a Discord message and a hope.

Staffed by people who fly

Grade 3 and up
