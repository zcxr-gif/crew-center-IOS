Progression

From Cadet to the left seat

Rank does two things here. It releases more of the fleet, and it raises the longest sector you are allowed to file. That second one is the part that makes the ladder mean something: a Cadet flies Guadalajara, and Tokyo is a sector no rank below Senior Captain may book at all.

How the ladder is applied

Promotion is arithmetic, not a favour

Hours are credited the moment a flight report is validated. Cross a threshold and the promotion lands in your crew center — no application, no waiting for somebody to notice you.
